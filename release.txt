Inverter Control v1.23.3 fixes a background telemetry polling exception when an optional D-Bus service disappears and its CLI fallback returns no result.

The missing reply now uses the existing zero fallback instead of raising an AttributeError. Other PV yield reads and subsequent battery-energy polling complete, and values recover on a later successful poll. Existing service backoff still applies.

The runtime change is a two-line guard. Device-local configuration, grid-loss handling, control policy and limits are preserved.

Validation: regression tests cover missing and empty CLI replies with and without native D-Bus, service backoff, continued polling of another PV service and battery energy, and recovery on a later poll.
