## v1.17.0

### Creep Correction for Grid-Zero Deadband

When system sits in deadband zone (grid power between -50W and 80W) but consistently imports/exports 30-100W, setpoint was frozen — caused chronic grid offset.

New creep integrator slowly accumulates error and nudges setpoint toward zero:

- `CREEP_RATE = 0.5` — watts accumulated per cycle while in deadband
- `CREEP_MAX = 100.0` — maximum creep correction cap
- Creep resets automatically when grid leaves deadband
- Console flag shows creep accumulator: `[~45]` = 45W accumulated
